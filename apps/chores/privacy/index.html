Chores Go Round Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This policy covers the Chores Go Round Android app (com.nuvopilot.chores, version 1.1.0 and later) and email you send us about it. Chores Go Round is published on Google Play by nuvopilot and operated by LABONDANCE Co., Ltd. (nuvopilot), Republic of Korea. Privacy questions: support@nuvopilot.com.

What the app keeps on your phone

The household you set up: the names and color slots of the people you add, each shared job with its effort points and turn order, and a turn log showing who was up, who did the job or covered it, the points credited, any reason you typed and the day and time. If you tap Bring in 1.0 routines, the app reads the older version's routines file without changing it.

All of this is stored in the app's private storage. There is no account and no Chores Go Round server, so these records never reach us. Names you type may belong to other people in your household; only enter names they are comfortable with.

Backup files

Export backup in Settings writes people, jobs and the turn log to a JSON file at a place you pick in Android's file picker. If that place is a cloud drive, its provider stores the file under its own terms. Import backup reads a file you choose and only adds entries that are not already on the phone.

Permissions

Chores Go Round itself uses internet and network-state access for Google ads and consent; the Google Mobile Ads SDK adds Android's ad services permissions (ad ID, attribution, topics) plus wake-lock and foreground-service access used by its background work library. It does not ask for notifications, contacts, location, camera, photos or storage access. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of app data are turned off.

Ads by Google

Chores Go Round shows a Google AdMob banner only on the Balance screen. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks consent status each time the app starts, so the app can use the network before any banner appears. Google documents that its Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate general location;
  • app interactions such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostics such as launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • device identifiers such as the app set ID and, where available, identifiers of accounts signed in on the device.

Chores Go Round does not request the Google Play services advertising ID permission, but the Google Mobile Ads SDK adds Android Privacy Sandbox permissions (ad ID, attribution and topics), so Google may still receive an advertising identifier on some devices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit with TLS. People's names, job names and turn history are not added to ad requests by the app. On the phone, the app also keeps simple counts of ad events (for example requests, loads and impressions) with no record content.



When you email support

An email to support@nuvopilot.com gives us your address, your message and anything you attach. We use it only to answer you and follow up on the same issue. Mail is received and answered through Zoho Mail.

Support is AI-assisted. We may submit the text of your inquiry and information you choose to include to Anthropic's Claude, an AI service we use to understand the issue and draft a reply. This processing happens on Anthropic's systems, not on your phone. App records on your phone are never sent automatically, and mailbox messages and attachments are not forwarded automatically. Anthropic handles submitted text under its own terms and privacy policy; we do not promise a particular retention period or training setting.


Please leave out passwords, verification codes, payment details and full backup files. We keep support mail only as long as needed to resolve your question or meet a legal duty, and you can ask us to delete it by writing to the same address.

Your choices and deleting data

Removing a person or a job takes them out of future turns; their name stays in past turns, past weekly balances and any backup you exported. To erase everything from the phone, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Delete exported backups where you saved them.

Where ad consent applies to you, open Settings and tap Ad privacy options under Privacy and ads. Android's own ad privacy settings are also available. Google manages the data it receives; clearing the app does not delete Google's data.

Children

Chores Go Round is made for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, write to support@nuvopilot.com and we will delete that correspondence.

Security

Records stay in storage that other apps cannot read. Keep exported files private, because they hold your records in plain JSON. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure.

Changes and contact

When this app's data handling changes we will update this page and its effective date before or with the app update. Contact support@nuvopilot.com with the app name and your question; you never need to send your records.
