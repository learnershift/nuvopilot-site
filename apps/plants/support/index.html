Frondlog for Houseplant Care · Android

Frondlog Support

Photo journal and care log

Frondlog 1.1.0 keeps a page for each plant with its room, light, pot, soil, notes and a care plan you can adjust. Tap Water, Fertilize, Repot, Prune or Mist on that page to log care with a date and an optional note. The History list on each plant can be narrowed to one care type or to photos.

To add a picture, tap Add photo and choose it in the Android photo picker; on older Android versions the file chooser opens instead. Frondlog never asks for access to your whole photo library. It stores a resized copy inside its own storage on this phone and leaves out the original picture's metadata, such as location. Moving a plant to Past plants keeps its history and photos, and you can bring it back to the journal later.

Watering-check reminders

Reminders stay off until you turn them on in Settings and pick an hour between 6 AM and 9 PM. On Android 13 and later, the notification permission prompt appears only at that point. If you deny the permission, reminders stay off; you can try again or allow notifications for Frondlog in Android settings.

About once a day near your chosen hour, a single quiet notification names up to two plants whose care plan says the soil is due for a check, plus how many more are due. It is a nudge to feel the soil, not an instruction to water. Android may shift the exact time a little to save battery. A plant is included when its care plan has a watering interval and you have logged at least one watering for it; past plants are left out. After your phone restarts, open Frondlog one time so reminders pick up again.

Backup file and restore

Save backup file writes one JSON file, to a place you pick, with every plant, profile, care plan and care entry, past plants included. Photos are never part of that file: they live only inside the app, so uninstalling Frondlog or clearing its storage removes them. Restore from file checks the backup first and then asks before it replaces everything currently in your journal. Photos already on the phone stay only if their plant is also in the restored file; pictures of plants missing from it are deleted. Backups made by the first version of Frondlog can still be restored.

Your journal stays on this phone: there is no Frondlog account, Frondlog makes no cloud copy and nothing syncs between devices. A saved backup file is separate from the app, so uninstalling Frondlog leaves that file wherever you put it.

Getting help with Frondlog

Questions about Frondlog or how it handles your data can go to support@nuvopilot.com. Please mention Frondlog and add the app version, your phone model, its Android version and what you tapped right before the problem showed up.

Leave out passwords, sign-in or verification codes, card details and your backup file, and crop or blur anything private in a screenshot before attaching it. An email reveals your address and everything you write to the people and mail providers that handle it.

Because Frondlog keeps your plants and photos only on your phone, support has no way to view, recover or erase them for you. Frondlog works without creating an account.
