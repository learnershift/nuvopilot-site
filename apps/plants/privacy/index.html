Frondlog Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This policy covers the Frondlog Android app (com.nuvopilot.plants, version 1.1.0 and later; earlier versions used a different name) and email you send us about it. Frondlog is published on Google Play by nuvopilot and operated by LABONDANCE Co., Ltd. (nuvopilot), Republic of Korea. Privacy questions: support@nuvopilot.com.

Your plant journal

Frondlog keeps each plant's name, room, notes, light, pot and soil profile, care preset and care plan, typed care entries (watering, feeding, repotting, pruning, misting) with dates and notes, and photo entries. Reminder settings and a short plan of plant names with their next soil-check dates are stored for the reminder check.

The journal is stored in the app's private storage and is not uploaded to us or added to ad requests.

Photos

You choose photos with Android's photo picker (or the document picker on older phones); Frondlog does not ask for gallery or storage permission. Each chosen picture is copied into the app as a resized JPEG, which drops the original file's metadata such as GPS location. Photos are never uploaded and are not included in backup files. Deleting a photo in Frondlog removes only the app's copy; the original stays in your gallery.

Watering-check reminders

Reminders are off until you switch them on; Android 13 and later then asks for notification permission. A daily check on the phone uses plant names and dates to post at most one notification per day around the hour you pick. After a phone restart, reminders resume once you open the app again.

Backups

Backups are JSON files (plants, profiles, care plans and care entries, without photos) that you save or open with Android's document picker. Restoring replaces the journal after you confirm; photos of plants not in that file are deleted, as the confirmation says.

Permissions

Frondlog itself uses internet and network-state access for Google ads and consent, and notification permission for optional reminders. The Google Mobile Ads SDK adds Android's ad services permissions (ad ID, attribution, topics), and the WorkManager library used by the ads SDK and by the app's reminders adds wake-lock and foreground-service access. It requests no camera, gallery, storage, location or contacts permission. Android cloud backup of app data is turned off; a direct device-to-device transfer while setting up a new phone may still copy app data.

Ads by Google

Frondlog shows a Google AdMob banner only on the Past plants screen. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks consent status each time the app starts, so the app can use the network before any banner appears. Google documents that its Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate general location;
  • app interactions such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostics such as launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • device identifiers such as the app set ID and, where available, identifiers of accounts signed in on the device.

Frondlog does not request the Google Play services advertising ID permission, but the Google Mobile Ads SDK adds Android Privacy Sandbox permissions (ad ID, attribution and topics), so Google may still receive an advertising identifier on some devices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit with TLS. Plant names, notes and photos are not added to ad requests by the app. On the phone, the app also keeps simple counts of ad events (for example requests, loads and impressions) with no record content.



When you email support

An email to support@nuvopilot.com gives us your address, your message and anything you attach. We use it only to answer you and follow up on the same issue. Mail is received and answered through Zoho Mail.

Support is AI-assisted. We may submit the text of your inquiry and information you choose to include to Anthropic's Claude, an AI service we use to understand the issue and draft a reply. This processing happens on Anthropic's systems, not on your phone. App records on your phone are never sent automatically, and mailbox messages and attachments are not forwarded automatically. Anthropic handles submitted text under its own terms and privacy policy; we do not promise a particular retention period or training setting.


Please leave out passwords, verification codes, payment details and full backup files. We keep support mail only as long as needed to resolve your question or meet a legal duty, and you can ask us to delete it by writing to the same address.

Your choices and deleting data

Delete single care entries or photos in the app, or move a plant to Past plants to keep its history. Clearing Frondlog's storage or uninstalling removes every record and photo from the phone. Backup files stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Turn reminders off in the app or block notifications in Android Settings. Ad privacy options, when they apply, are under Settings › Privacy and ads. Google keeps the data it receives under its own policies.

Children

Frondlog is made for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, write to support@nuvopilot.com and we will delete that correspondence.

Security

Records stay in storage that other apps cannot read. Keep exported files private, because they hold your records in plain JSON. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure.

Changes and contact

When this app's data handling changes we will update this page and its effective date before or with the app update. Contact support@nuvopilot.com with the app name and your question; you never need to send your records.
