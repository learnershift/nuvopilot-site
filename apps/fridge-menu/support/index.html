Fridge Menu (냉장고 메뉴) is a local-first Android app that keeps ingredient lists, favorites, and recent menus on the user's device.

Contact

Email: support@nuvopilot.com

Support is available for app usage, local data, privacy, and store-listing questions.

Before Contacting Support

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment information, or other sensitive information in support messages.

What To Include

  • App version
  • Device model and Android version
  • The screen or action where the issue occurred
  • Screenshots only if they contain no private or sensitive information

Privacy And Data Boundary

Fridge Menu has zero Android permissions and no network permission, account, analytics, tracking, advertising SDK, or external API. LABONDANCE Co., Ltd. does not receive the local app data and has no server copy.
