Fridge Menu

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-29

This policy applies to the Fridge Menu (냉장고 메뉴) Android app and its policy and support pages.

Data Stored Locally

Ingredient lists, favorites, and recent menus are stored only in browser or Android WebView storage on the user's device.

No Account Connection

Fridge Menu has no account or login and does not connect local app data to a developer-side user account.

No Server Upload Or Analytics

  • Zero Android permissions
  • No network permission
  • No server upload
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising SDK
  • No external or remote API

How Data Is Used

The app uses locally stored data on the device to display ingredient lists, favorites, and recent menus. LABONDANCE Co., Ltd. does not receive or process that on-device data.

Sharing

The app does not send local app data to LABONDANCE Co., Ltd. or share it with third parties.

Retention And Deletion

Local data remains on the device until the user removes it. The in-app “목록 비우기” action removes ingredients only. To remove ingredients, favorites, and history, clear the app's storage through the device operating system or uninstall the app. No developer-side server copy exists.

See the Delete Data page for deletion instructions.

Security

Fridge Menu limits developer-side exposure by keeping app data on the device and by not requesting network permission. LABONDANCE Co., Ltd. does not receive the local data and has no server copy.

User Control

Users control local data through the in-app ingredient clear action, the operating system's app-storage controls, and app uninstallation. Because there is no developer-side account or server copy, LABONDANCE Co., Ltd. cannot remotely access or delete the local device data.

Children

Fridge Menu is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Changes

Material changes will be announced in the in-app policy and at this public policy URL before they take effect. The effective date will be updated.

Important Limits

Fridge Menu provides flexible meal directions. They are not recipes, nutrition advice, allergy guidance, or food-safety information. Check ingredients and use your own judgment when cooking.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@nuvopilot.com. The privacy officer under Korea's Personal Information Protection Act is 송문길. People in the EEA may lodge a complaint with their local data-protection supervisory authority.

한국어 개인정보처리방침
