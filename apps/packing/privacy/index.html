Sock Math Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This policy covers the Sock Math Android app (com.nuvopilot.packing, version 1.1.0 and later) and email you send us about it. Sock Math is published on Google Play by nuvopilot and operated by LABONDANCE Co., Ltd. (nuvopilot), Republic of Korea. Privacy questions: support@nuvopilot.com.

Trip data on your phone

Sock Math stores trip names and departure dates, nights, climate, activities and laundry choices, the first names or nicknames you give travelers, bag names, each item with its quantity, owner, bag and packed mark, leave-home checks, packing rules and templates. A backup you start is staged in a private file first, and the app notes which trips have a reminder set.

Everything is kept in a private database inside the app. Traveler names are what you type and may refer to other people; nothing is uploaded to us.

Trip reminders

Reminders are optional. When you turn one on, Android 13 and later asks whether Sock Math may post notifications. The app sets one inexact local alarm for about 6 PM the evening before departure and shows the trip name and the number of unpacked items. After a restart the app re-creates its alarms from the saved trips (this is why it can run at boot). Turning the reminder off, archiving or deleting the trip cancels it. No server is involved.

Sharing a list and backups

Share list builds a plain-text copy of the trip (name, date, people, items, quantities, bags, packed marks and checks) and hands it to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. That app receives the text under its own policy. Backups are JSON files you save through the system file picker or copy as text; restoring one replaces all trips after you confirm.

Permissions

Sock Math itself uses internet and network-state access for Google ads and consent, notification permission for optional trip reminders, and the boot-completed signal to restore reminders. The Google Mobile Ads SDK adds Android's ad services permissions (ad ID, attribution, topics) plus wake-lock and foreground-service access for its background work library. Sock Math does not use exact alarms, contacts, location, camera, photos or storage permissions. Android cloud backup of app data is turned off; a direct device-to-device transfer while setting up a new phone may still copy app data.

Ads by Google

Sock Math shows a Google AdMob banner only on the Past trips list. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks consent status each time the app starts, so the app can use the network before any banner appears. Google documents that its Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate general location;
  • app interactions such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostics such as launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • device identifiers such as the app set ID and, where available, identifiers of accounts signed in on the device.

Sock Math does not request the Google Play services advertising ID permission, but the Google Mobile Ads SDK adds Android Privacy Sandbox permissions (ad ID, attribution and topics), so Google may still receive an advertising identifier on some devices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit with TLS. Trip details, traveler names and packing items are not added to ad requests by the app. On the phone, the app also keeps simple counts of ad events (for example requests, loads and impressions) with no record content.



When you email support

An email to support@nuvopilot.com gives us your address, your message and anything you attach. We use it only to answer you and follow up on the same issue. Mail is received and answered through Zoho Mail.

Support is AI-assisted. We may submit the text of your inquiry and information you choose to include to Anthropic's Claude, an AI service we use to understand the issue and draft a reply. This processing happens on Anthropic's systems, not on your phone. App records on your phone are never sent automatically, and mailbox messages and attachments are not forwarded automatically. Anthropic handles submitted text under its own terms and privacy policy; we do not promise a particular retention period or training setting.


Please leave out passwords, verification codes, payment details and full backup files. We keep support mail only as long as needed to resolve your question or meet a legal duty, and you can ask us to delete it by writing to the same address.

Your choices and deleting data

You can delete a trip and individual items, travelers, bags, groups, checks, rules and templates. Clearing Sock Math's storage or uninstalling removes the database, any staged backup and the reminder list. Exported files and text you shared stay wherever you put them.

Turn notifications off at any time in Android Settings. Where ad consent applies, open the Settings tab and tap Ad privacy options under Privacy and ads. Google keeps the data it receives under its own policies.

Children

Sock Math is made for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, write to support@nuvopilot.com and we will delete that correspondence.

Security

Records stay in storage that other apps cannot read. Keep exported files private, because they hold your records in plain JSON. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure.

Changes and contact

When this app's data handling changes we will update this page and its effective date before or with the app update. Contact support@nuvopilot.com with the app name and your question; you never need to send your records.
