NuvoPilot Packing Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy covers the NuvoPilot Packing Android app and support inquiries about it. The operator is LABONDANCE Co., Ltd. (nuvopilot), Republic of Korea. For privacy questions or support-message deletion requests, email support@nuvopilot.com.

Records on your device

Trip names and dates, packing templates, category and item names, quantities, packed status, and archive status. The app uses these records to organize packing lists and reusable templates.

Records are stored in the app's storage on your device. There is no app account or automatic record sync to us. Record contents are not included directly in advertising requests. The app also keeps local settings and diagnostic counts of app and ad events; these are separate from your record contents.

Backups you choose to save

Export saves a JSON copy to the location you choose in the file picker. Import reads the file you select. If you select cloud storage or share a file, that provider receives the copy under its own privacy rules. We do not receive your backup through the export feature. Backups can contain your private entries; keep them private.

Google advertising and consent

The app includes Google AdMob and Google's consent tools. Google collects and shares network addresses, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for ads, measurement, and fraud prevention. A network address can indicate your approximate location.

Advertising ID availability depends on Android settings and app configuration; its absence does not stop all SDK collection. Your consent choices affect advertising and data processing. Google describes SDK data transmission as protected with TLS encryption. This does not mean that every file or message is encrypted by this app.

For Google's handling and retention of advertising data, read Google's Privacy Policy and its advertising technologies explanation. To open a policy, select and copy the full address in its read-only field, paste it into your browser's address bar, and open it. On a computer, focus the field and press Ctrl+A (Command+A on Mac), then copy. On a phone, touch and hold the address, choose Select all, then Copy.



When you contact support

We receive your email address and the message, attachments, and device or app details you choose to send. We use this information to understand your question, respond, and resolve related issues. We use Zoho Mail to receive and reply to messages. Your email service and Zoho process the correspondence to deliver and store it.

Support is AI-assisted. Under our support policy effective October 14, 2026, we may submit the text of your inquiry and relevant information you voluntarily include, such as contact details or other personal information, to OpenAI through our existing ChatGPT/Codex workflow to understand the issue and draft a reply. This processing takes place on OpenAI-hosted services, not only on your device. App-local records are not automatically sent to OpenAI. This workflow does not automatically forward mailbox messages or attachments.

OpenAI processes submitted information under the terms and privacy policy applicable to the service and its settings. We do not promise a particular OpenAI retention period, training setting, or automatic deletion. Read OpenAI's Privacy Policy using the address below; select, copy, and open it in your browser as described above.


Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details, full backups, or unnecessary sensitive information. Remove private details from screenshots.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Archiving a trip keeps its records available to restore. Confirming a backup import replaces current trips and templates. Export current lists first if you want to keep them. Clear app storage to remove all local lists.

Local records remain until you remove them. To remove app-local data, use Android Settings to clear this app's storage or uninstall the app. Export first if you want to keep a copy. Support cannot remotely erase records on your device.

Exported files are separate copies and are not deleted by clearing storage, uninstalling, or changing an app record. Delete those copies separately in the folder or service where you saved or shared them. Any device backup or transfer copy is managed through its provider's controls.

Our support correspondence policy, effective from the date above, is to keep messages only as needed to answer the inquiry, resolve follow-up issues, or meet applicable legal retention requirements. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address above. If a legal requirement prevents deletion, we will explain the limitation. This policy does not promise a fixed automatic deletion schedule.

Use Ad privacy options in the app when that control is available to revisit consent choices. You can also use Android's advertising privacy controls. Google manages data it receives under its policies; removing local app records does not itself delete Google's data.

Data security

The app keeps your saved records within its app storage and sends exported copies only to a destination you choose. Protect your device and backups, and share only information needed for support. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Policy updates and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted at this address with an updated effective date. Contact support@nuvopilot.com with the app name and your privacy question. You do not need to send your local records to ask a question.
