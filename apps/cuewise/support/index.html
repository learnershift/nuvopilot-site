Cuewise Support

Updated October 20, 2026

Cuewise is an Android app for rehearsing lines: paste a script, pick your part, and hear the other parts read by your phone's own voice.

Write to us

Email support@nuvopilot.com. A person reads each message and answers from that address.

Replies are drafted with help from an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) and checked before they are sent. The Privacy Policy explains what that means for your message.

Helpful details

  • Your Cuewise version, shown in the app menu or on its Google Play page
  • Your phone model and Android version
  • What you tapped and what happened instead
  • A screenshot, if it shows no private script text you would rather keep to yourself

Please leave passwords, sign-in codes and card numbers out of your message. Cuewise never asks for them.

Common questions

Nothing is read aloud. Cuewise uses the text-to-speech engine already on your phone. Check that a speech engine and an English voice are installed in Android's text-to-speech settings, and that media volume is up.

Where are my scripts? They live only in Cuewise's storage on this phone. There is no account or cloud copy, so uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes them.

Removing your data. Delete a script inside the app, or clear Cuewise's storage in Android settings to remove everything at once. To remove an email you sent us, ask at the same address.
