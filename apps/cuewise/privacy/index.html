Cuewise Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Your scripts stay on this phone

Scripts you paste, their names, the part you pick, your Got it and Missed marks, and your voice speed are saved only in this app's storage on your phone. Cuewise has no account and no server of its own. We do not receive, sell, or share your scripts.

Cuewise turns off Android cloud backup, so your scripts are not copied to Google Drive or any other cloud backup. If you set up a new phone with Android's phone-to-phone transfer, your scripts may be moved to the new phone directly. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, your scripts are deleted.

Reading lines aloud

When you tap Hear on a line, Cuewise uses the text-to-speech engine already on your phone to read that one line. Only the text of that line is passed to the engine, on your phone. Nothing is read unless you tap. Cuewise does not record audio and does not ask for microphone access. If your phone's speech engine is provided by another company, its own privacy terms apply to how that engine works.

Ads

Cuewise shows one banner ad from Google AdMob at the top of two list pages, All scripts and Missed lines, marked "Ad". The script page you practice on has no ads, and Cuewise shows no full-screen ads. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as your IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions, and may use cookies or similar identifiers. Cuewise keeps the advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) that the Mobile Ads SDK requests, together with the Android Privacy Sandbox permissions it adds for ad ID, attribution and topics, so Google can read your phone's advertising ID to pick and count ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Your scripts are never sent to the ads SDK.

Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalized ads, and you can change that choice later with "Ad privacy choices" in the Cuewise menu. Google's use of information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

Cuewise is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Who runs Cuewise

Cuewise (com.nuvopilot.cuewise) is published on Google Play under the nuvopilot name, which belongs to LABONDANCE Co., Ltd., a company in the Republic of Korea. For privacy questions or requests, write to support@nuvopilot.com.

When you write to that address, we get your email address, your message and any attachments, and we use them only to answer you and follow up on the same question. Our mailbox runs on Zoho Mail. Ask us and we will delete your correspondence.

Support is AI-assisted: we may give the text of your message, and anything you chose to include in it, to Anthropic (Claude), an AI service that helps us understand the question and draft a reply on its own servers. That provider handles the text under its own terms and privacy policy (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy); we do not promise a particular retention period or training setting. Scripts and other data stored in Cuewise on your phone are never sent automatically, and attachments are not forwarded automatically.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on the store listing with a new effective date. This policy is also posted at https://nuvopilot.com/apps/cuewise/privacy/, and help with the app is at https://nuvopilot.com/apps/cuewise/support/.
