Backups and records

Chapterwell keeps the books, pages and reading dates you enter, plus your yearly reading goal, in the app's private storage on this device. There is no account, no ads and no network access, so we cannot see, read remotely or recover your shelf. Archiving a book keeps it.

Save a JSON backup to a location you choose with Android's file picker, and import a backup you select. Import keeps existing books and refuses conflicting copies. Chapterwell turns off Android automatic backup and asks Android to exclude its data from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so save your own backup before changing phones or clearing storage. Backup files are separate copies: clearing app storage or uninstalling does not delete them. Delete them where you saved them.

Contact support

For app usage or privacy questions, email support@nuvopilot.com. Include the app name, version, device model, Android version, and the steps that caused the issue.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment information, a full backup, or unnecessary personal information. Remove private details from screenshots. Email shares your address and the message you choose to send with its recipients and the email services involved.

Support cannot remotely clear records stored only on your device. Chapterwell does not require an app account.
