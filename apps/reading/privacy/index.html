Chapterwell Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This policy covers the Chapterwell Android app (com.nuvopilot.chapterwell, version 1.1.0 and later) and email you send us about it. Chapterwell is published on Google Play by nuvopilot and operated by LABONDANCE Co., Ltd. (nuvopilot), Republic of Korea. Privacy questions: support@nuvopilot.com.

Summary

Chapterwell does not collect or share personal or usage data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, requests no Android permissions and cannot make network requests.

Your shelf on this device

Chapterwell stores the book titles, authors, page counts, current pages, logged pages and reading dates, archive status and yearly reading goal you enter, plus the book you have open and an unsaved editor draft, in the app's private storage. Stats, streaks, goal progress and estimated finish dates are calculated on the device. We cannot see, read remotely or recover this information.

Backup and import

You can save a JSON backup to a location you choose with Android's file picker and import a backup you select. A backup holds your reading history and yearly goal, so keep it private. If you pick a cloud provider, it handles the file under its own terms. Import keeps existing books and refuses conflicting copies.

Chapterwell turns off Android automatic backup and asks Android to exclude its data from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Save your own backup before changing phones.

When you email support

An email to support@nuvopilot.com gives us your address, your message and anything you attach. We use it only to answer you and follow up on the same issue. Mail is received and answered through Zoho Mail.

Support is AI-assisted. We may submit the text of your inquiry and information you choose to include to Anthropic's Claude, an AI service we use to understand the issue and draft a reply. This processing happens on Anthropic's systems, not on your phone. App records on your phone are never sent automatically, and mailbox messages and attachments are not forwarded automatically. Anthropic handles submitted text under its own terms and privacy policy; we do not promise a particular retention period or training setting.


Please leave out passwords, verification codes, payment details and full backup files. We keep support mail only as long as needed to resolve your question or meet a legal duty, and you can ask us to delete it by writing to the same address.

Your choices and deleting data

Your shelf stays until you clear Chapterwell's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Archiving a book keeps it. Clearing cannot be undone without a backup, and backup files you saved elsewhere remain until you delete them there.

Children

Chapterwell is made for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, write to support@nuvopilot.com and we will delete that correspondence.

Security

Records stay in storage that other apps cannot read. Keep exported files private, because they hold your records in plain JSON. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure.

Changes and contact

When this app's data handling changes we will update this page and its effective date before or with the app update. Contact support@nuvopilot.com with the app name and your question; you never need to send your records.
