Holdclock Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This page explains how Holdclock, a hangboard interval timer for Android (version 1.0.0 and later), handles information. Holdclock is published by LABONDANCE Co., Ltd. (nuvopilot) in the Republic of Korea. Send questions about this policy to support@nuvopilot.com.

What stays on your phone

Holdclock saves your timing plans, the grip names you type, cue settings, and a training log. Each log entry holds when the workout started and ended, the plan name, hangs completed out of hangs planned, whether you finished or stopped early, seconds on the board, and an optional short note. Holdclock keeps your most recent 500 sessions.

Holdclock also keeps a checkpoint of a workout in progress, the date the last full-screen ad appeared, whether you have read the safety note, and the ad choices saved by Google's consent tool.

Everything listed here is kept in Holdclock's private storage on your phone. Holdclock has no sign-in, no account, and no server of its own, so the developer never receives these entries. The app's screens load from files packaged inside the app rather than from the web.

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for Holdclock. Setting up a new phone therefore starts with an empty log.

Removing your data

To erase your plans and log in one step, open Settings in the app, choose Erase plans and log, and confirm; the two starter plans and default cue settings then come back. Clearing Holdclock's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, also removes them. Erase plans and log does not change your ad choices; use Ad privacy choices, when Settings shows it, for those. Because nothing is copied to us, we cannot recover or delete entries on your behalf.

Ads from Google AdMob

Holdclock is free to use and is paid for by Google AdMob ads. A banner can appear along the bottom of the start screen and the log screen. A full-screen ad may appear at most once per day, after you close the summary of a workout you finished. Ads never appear during a session, even while it is paused.

To show ads, measure them, personalize them unless you opt out, and detect fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your IP address, device identifiers such as the advertising ID and the app set ID, device and app details, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. Google may share this data with its advertising partners as its policies describe. An IP address can reveal a rough location.

Holdclock declares Google's advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can use your phone's advertising ID for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. The SDK also declares Android's ad services permissions for the ad ID, attribution reporting, and topics, and it may use them on phones that support those features. In your phone's settings you can reset or delete the advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads.

Your plans, grip names, notes, and log are never passed to Google or any other party by Holdclock.

If you live in a US state whose privacy law is covered by Google's privacy message, Settings in the app shows Ad privacy choices. It opens Google's message, where you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for personalized ads, and you can return there to change your choice at any time.

Google explains its own use of this data in the two documents below. Copy an address into your browser to open it.



Sound, speech, and vibration

Beeps, vibration, and the spoken countdown are all produced on the phone. Spoken cues use the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone, which receives only built-in phrases and the grip names you entered and handles them under its own settings. Holdclock does not ask for microphone, camera, location, contacts, or file access.

Training and health

Holdclock only keeps time for the plan you enter. It offers no medical advice or diagnosis and does not recommend loads. Your log is not used for advertising.

Children

The app is intended for adult climbers who already train on a hangboard and is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if you think a child has emailed us, tell us and we will delete that message.

Emailing support

If you write to support, we get your email address and whatever message, screenshots, or device details you include, and we use them only to answer you and follow up. Mail is received and answered through Zoho Mail. Support messages may be processed with third-party tools, which can include AI services, for that purpose only; those tools may handle your message and attachments on their providers' servers. Data inside the app is never sent automatically.

Please leave out passwords, codes, payment details, and anything sensitive that the question does not need. We keep support mail only as long as needed to resolve your request or meet legal obligations, and you can ask us by email to delete it.

Security

Google states that the ads SDK sends its data over encrypted connections. No method of storing or moving data is perfectly secure, so keep your phone locked and up to date.

Changes and contact

When this policy is revised, the new text replaces this page and the date at the top changes. If you have a privacy question, write to support@nuvopilot.com and mention Holdclock.
