Holdclock Support

Holdclock for Android, version 1.0.0

Running a session

Tap Edit timing to set the hang, rest between reps, reps per set, sets, rest between sets, and get-ready countdown, then press Start once. Holdclock moves through every hang and rest by itself and counts reps from the clock, so you never need to tap the screen mid-set.

You can give each set a grip name. During the countdown or the rest before each set, the voice tells you which set is next and the grip you named for it.

Common questions

I hear no spoken cues. First check that Voice is switched on in Holdclock's Settings. Holdclock speaks through your phone's text-to-speech engine in US English, so install or turn on an English (United States) voice in Android's text-to-speech settings, then raise your phone's media and system volume.

The timer paused without me. Leaving for another app, taking a call, or turning the display off pauses the session. Another app that takes over the sound during or just after a cue can pause it too. Return to Holdclock and tap Resume.

Can I end early? Press Stop and confirm. The hangs you already finished are saved to your log as a stopped-early session. To cut only the current rest or countdown short, tap Skip rest or Skip countdown.

Will my log move to a new phone? No. Holdclock is excluded from Android backup and transfer, so a new install starts empty.

How do I wipe my plans and log? Go to Settings, choose Erase plans and log, and confirm. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling also removes them.

When do ads show? A banner may sit at the bottom of the start and log screens. At most one full-screen ad a day can follow when you close the summary of a finished workout, and none appear mid-session. If you live in a US state covered by Google's privacy message, use Settings > Ad privacy choices to review or change your ad choices.

Holdclock is a timer, not a coach. Warm up, stop if anything hurts, and ask a qualified coach or clinician about training loads.

Contact support

Email support@nuvopilot.com with Holdclock in the subject line. Tell us your app version (shown at the bottom of Settings), phone model, Android version, and what happened just before the problem.

Leave passwords, codes, and payment details out of your message, and crop personal details from any screenshot. Holdclock has no account, so support cannot see or restore data kept on your phone. Our privacy policy explains how support email is handled.
