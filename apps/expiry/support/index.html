Stubtimer Support

This page covers Stubtimer 1.1.0 for Android. Version 1.1.0 renamed the app; purchases saved under its earlier name are kept after the update.

Entering dates

Type the return and warranty end dates printed on your receipt or given by the seller, or use the +14, +30 and +90 day and +1 to +3 year buttons, which count from the purchase date. Either date may stay empty. Stubtimer does not look up store policies and cannot say whether a return or a warranty claim will be accepted.

Reminders

Reminders start switched off; switch them on in Settings. Pick any of 7, 3 and 1 days before; the last day of each window always gets its own notice. On Android 13 or later the system asks for notification permission at that moment, and if you decline, reminders stay off.

A once-a-day check on the phone decides which notices to show, and Android may run it late to save battery, so a notice can arrive after the hour you expect. If Settings says notifications are blocked, use its Open notification settings button. Marking a purchase done ends its reminders.

Receipt photos

Each purchase can hold up to three images, such as the paper receipt, an order email screenshot or a warranty card. Choose Add from photos or Take photo on the purchase screen. Stubtimer keeps its own JPEG copy in app storage without location data and does not upload it. Tap a photo to view or remove it.

Calendar files

Settings can export every upcoming deadline as one .ics file, and a purchase screen can export just that item. Open the saved file with a calendar app that imports .ics files. Stubtimer has no calendar permission, so later edits in the app do not change events you already imported.

Backups and restoring

Export backup writes a JSON file with purchase names, dates, notes and done marks. Receipt photos and reminder choices are not in that file, and the app cannot export photos. A picture taken with Take photo is saved straight into Stubtimer and may exist nowhere else, while an image chosen with Add from photos still has its original wherever it came from; clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the Stubtimer copies.

Restore backup asks for confirmation, then swaps every saved purchase and unsaved form draft for the file's contents. Photos remain only for purchases whose entries match the file. Backups made before the rename still restore.

Done, moving back and deleting

Mark as done moves a purchase to the Done list, and Move back to timeline brings it back. Delete purchase asks first and then erases that purchase with its notes and photos. Files you exported earlier stay wherever you saved them until you remove them there.

Contact support

Write to support@nuvopilot.com with "Stubtimer" in the subject. Tell us the app version shown on the phone's app info screen, your phone model and Android version, plus what you tapped before the problem appeared.

Please keep receipts, order numbers, backup files and sign-in codes out of your message, and blur names or addresses in any screenshot. How support mail is handled, including AI-assisted replies, is described in the Stubtimer privacy policy.

Because your purchase list is kept on your phone alone, we cannot view, recover or erase it for you. Stubtimer has no sign-in.
