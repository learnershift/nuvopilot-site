Backups and records

Record purchase dates and seller-provided return and warranty deadlines. Leave unknown deadlines blank. The app does not determine eligibility or send notifications. Export includes saved items, not editor drafts. Confirmed import replaces saved items and clears drafts. Delete item removes that saved item after confirmation; Archive is reversible.

Saved records are kept on this device without automatic sync. Exported files are separate copies: clearing app storage or uninstalling does not delete them. Manage copies in the document provider or folder where you saved them. Before clearing storage, keep a backup you can reopen.

Contact support

For app usage or privacy questions, email support@nuvopilot.com. Include the app name, version, device model, Android version, and the steps that caused the issue.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment information, a full backup, or unnecessary personal information. Remove private details from screenshots. Email shares your address and the message you choose to send with its recipients and the email services involved.

Support cannot remotely clear records stored only on your device. These apps do not require an app account.
