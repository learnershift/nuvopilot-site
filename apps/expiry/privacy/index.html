Stubtimer Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This policy covers the Stubtimer Android app (com.nuvopilot.expiry, version 1.1.0 and later; earlier versions used a different name) and email you send us about it. Stubtimer is published on Google Play by nuvopilot and operated by LABONDANCE Co., Ltd. (nuvopilot), Republic of Korea. Privacy questions: support@nuvopilot.com.

Purchases on your phone

Stubtimer saves purchase names, purchase dates, return and warranty end dates, notes, the done mark and links to receipt photos in a private database, plus reminder settings, chosen lead days and a note of reminders already shown. There is no account; these records are not sent to us or anyone else.

Receipt photos

Add up to three photos per purchase with Android's photo picker (or the file picker on older Android), or take one with your own camera app, which Stubtimer opens through Android with a one-time file link. Stubtimer stores a new JPEG copy without hidden details such as GPS location. It requests no camera, photo or storage permission. Photos are not uploaded and are not included in backups.

Deadline reminders

If you turn reminders on, Android 13 and later asks for notification permission. Once a day the phone checks your saved dates and may notify you before a return window or warranty ends and on the last day; the lock screen shows only a generic line. The check is re-armed after a restart. No server is involved, and turning reminders off stops it.

Calendar files and backups

Export upcoming deadlines saves an .ics calendar file (Add to calendar does the same for one purchase), and Export backup saves a JSON file, each to a place you choose in Android's document picker. Stubtimer never reads or changes your calendars. The storage or calendar app you pick handles the file under its own policy.

Permissions

Stubtimer itself uses internet and network-state access for Google ads and consent, notification permission for optional reminders, and the boot-completed signal to re-arm the daily check. The Google Mobile Ads SDK adds Android's ad services permissions (ad ID, attribution, topics), and the WorkManager library used by the ads SDK and by the app's reminders adds wake-lock and foreground-service access. It requests no camera, photo, storage, calendar, location or contacts permission. Android cloud backup of app data is turned off; a direct device-to-device transfer while setting up a new phone may still copy app data.

Ads by Google

Stubtimer shows a Google AdMob banner only on the Done list. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks consent status each time the app starts, so the app can use the network before any banner appears. Google documents that its Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate general location;
  • app interactions such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostics such as launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • device identifiers such as the app set ID and, where available, identifiers of accounts signed in on the device.

Stubtimer does not request the Google Play services advertising ID permission, but the Google Mobile Ads SDK adds Android Privacy Sandbox permissions (ad ID, attribution and topics), so Google may still receive an advertising identifier on some devices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit with TLS. Purchases, photos and reminder data are not added to ad requests by the app. On the phone, the app also keeps simple counts of ad events (for example requests, loads and impressions) with no record content.



When you email support

An email to support@nuvopilot.com gives us your address, your message and anything you attach. We use it only to answer you and follow up on the same issue. Mail is received and answered through Zoho Mail.

Support is AI-assisted. We may submit the text of your inquiry and information you choose to include to Anthropic's Claude, an AI service we use to understand the issue and draft a reply. This processing happens on Anthropic's systems, not on your phone. App records on your phone are never sent automatically, and mailbox messages and attachments are not forwarded automatically. Anthropic handles submitted text under its own terms and privacy policy; we do not promise a particular retention period or training setting.


Please leave out passwords, verification codes, payment details and full backup files. We keep support mail only as long as needed to resolve your question or meet a legal duty, and you can ask us to delete it by writing to the same address.

Your choices and deleting data

Deleting a purchase also deletes its photos, and you can remove a single photo. Restoring a backup replaces all purchases and keeps photos only for purchases that match. Clearing Stubtimer's storage or uninstalling erases everything it stored; exported .ics and JSON files must be deleted where you saved them.

Block notifications any time in Android Settings. Ad privacy options, when they apply, are in Settings under Privacy and ads. Google keeps the data it receives under its own policies.

Children

Stubtimer is made for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, write to support@nuvopilot.com and we will delete that correspondence.

Security

Records stay in storage that other apps cannot read. Keep exported files private, because they hold your records in plain JSON. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure.

Changes and contact

When this app's data handling changes we will update this page and its effective date before or with the app update. Contact support@nuvopilot.com with the app name and your question; you never need to send your records.
