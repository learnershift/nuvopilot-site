Rollwright · Android

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Rollwright is a pipe offset calculator for simple and rolling offsets, published by nuvopilot. Its Google Play listing is titled Rolling Pipe Offset Calculator. This page describes what happens to information while you use the Android app. The operator is LABONDANCE Co., Ltd. (nuvopilot), Republic of Korea.

What stays on your phone

Rise, roll, fitting angle, pipe size, take-off and joint gap values you type, the unit you pick, and every cut you save with its optional note and save date live in the app's own storage on the phone.

To space out full-screen ads, the app also counts saves, counts ads shown today and records when the last one appeared. Those counters stay in the same on-phone storage.

nuvopilot operates no server for Rollwright, so none of these values reach us. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this storage, which means a new phone starts with an empty cut sheet.

Ads from Google

The free version carries Google AdMob banner and full-screen ads. Where the law asks for consent, Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your choice in a consent message.

Google's ad software inside the app may process your IP address, from which an approximate location can be estimated, plus device and app identifiers such as the advertising ID, ad interactions and diagnostic data. Google uses this to serve and measure ads and to stop ad fraud.

Pipe measurements, notes and the cut sheet are never placed in ad requests. Ad traffic travels over encrypted connections.

Google explains its handling of this data in its privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and in its page on partner sites and apps (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Copy either address into a browser to read it.

Your ad choices

  • Where consent applies, the app shows an "Ad privacy options" link that reopens Google's consent form.
  • Android settings let you reset or delete the advertising ID.
  • Removing the app's data on the phone does not erase data Google already received; Google's own controls cover that.

Permissions

Rollwright's own code declares no permissions. The Google Mobile Ads SDK adds these, and Android grants all of them at install time without a prompt:

  • Internet and network-state access, used to fetch ads
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID)
  • Android ad services for ad ID, attribution and topics (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS), which the ad SDK can use on devices that support them
  • Wake lock and a foreground service for the SDK's background tasks

The app never requests camera, location, contacts, files, microphone or notification access.

Sharing a cut sheet

Tapping "Share as text" hands the cut sheet to the app you choose in Android's share menu. After that, the receiving app's own policy governs the copy.

Deleting cuts

Remove a single cut, or clear the whole cut sheet, from inside the app. Clearing Rollwright's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling it, wipes everything the app kept. Because no copy exists on our side, nuvopilot has nothing to erase remotely.

Emailing us

A message to support gives us your email address and whatever you choose to write or attach. We use it only to answer you. You never need to send saved cuts or measurements to ask a privacy question.

Our support inbox runs on Zoho Mail. Zoho stores the messages you send there and carries our replies back to you.

We draft answers with help from AI. To make sense of a question and write a reply, we may paste the text of your message, along with anything you put in it yourself such as your name or contact details, into Anthropic's Claude as part of our Claude Code workflow. Anthropic handles that text on its own servers, away from your phone. Rollwright never passes anything to Anthropic by itself, and incoming mail is not relayed there automatically.

What Anthropic does with pasted text depends on the terms and settings of the service we use. We cannot promise how long Anthropic keeps it or whether it is used to train models. Anthropic's privacy policy is at https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy; paste that address into a browser to read it.
