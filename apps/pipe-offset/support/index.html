Support

Updated: October 21, 2026

Rollwright works out the pipe length between two fittings when a run has to jog around a beam, a duct or another line. On Google Play it is listed as Rolling Pipe Offset Calculator.

Common questions

  • The cut length looks wrong. Check that take-off and joint gap match the fittings in your hand, and that the unit switch (in or mm) is the one you measured in.
  • Simple or rolling? Choose a simple offset for a jog in one plane. Choose Rolling offset when the line also moves sideways, then enter the roll center to center.
  • Where did my saved cuts go? Cuts are kept only on the phone that saved them. They are not backed up to the cloud and do not move to a new phone, so use "Share as text" to keep a copy elsewhere.
  • How do I remove cuts? Delete one cut or clear the cut sheet in the app. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling, removes everything.
  • Can I change my ad consent? Where consent applies, open "Ad privacy options" in the app. The advertising ID can be reset or deleted in Android settings.

Always check a calculated cut against the job and your own trade judgment before cutting pipe.

Contact

Email support@nuvopilot.com with "Rollwright" in the subject. We answer questions about the calculator, saved cuts, ads and privacy.

Mail to that address is kept in Zoho Mail, and replies may be drafted with Anthropic's Claude. See Emailing us in the privacy policy for what that means for your message.

A useful report says:

  • which Rollwright version you have (shown on the Play Store page)
  • the phone model and Android version
  • the rise, roll, angle and unit you entered, and the result you expected

Leave out passwords, payment details and anything you would not want stored in a mailbox. If you attach a screenshot, crop out notes or names first.

Privacy in short

The app has no account, and nuvopilot runs no server for it. Ads come from Google AdMob, which processes some device and ad data as described in the Rollwright Privacy Policy. Support cannot see or erase cuts stored on your phone.
