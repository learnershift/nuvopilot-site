Tinkrow Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This policy covers the Tinkrow Android app (com.nuvopilot.knitting, version 1.1.0 and later) and email you send us about it. Tinkrow is published on Google Play by nuvopilot and operated by LABONDANCE Co., Ltd. (nuvopilot), Republic of Korea. Privacy questions: support@nuvopilot.com.

Knitting records on your phone

For each project Tinkrow saves the name, notes, optional target rows, rows done, pattern repeat length, position and repeats finished, the optional stitch count, up to 20 undo steps, up to 100 row instructions and whether the project is archived. It also remembers three settings: counting with the volume buttons, vibration for row instructions and keeping the screen on.

Records live in a private file inside the app. Volume-button counting, the screen-on option and vibration run on the phone and need no extra permission. Nothing you enter is sent to us.

Saving and loading backups

Save backup file writes a JSON copy of your projects to a place you choose in Android's document picker; that storage service keeps it under its own rules. Load backup file replaces every project after you confirm, and Tinkrow keeps the earlier projects as an on-phone before-replace copy that you can save from Settings.

Permissions

Tinkrow itself uses internet and network-state access for Google ads and consent; the Google Mobile Ads SDK adds Android's ad services permissions (ad ID, attribution, topics) plus wake-lock and foreground-service access used by its background work library. It requests no notification, contacts, location, camera, photo or storage permission. Android cloud backup of app data is turned off; a direct device-to-device transfer while setting up a new phone may still copy app data.

Ads by Google

Tinkrow shows a Google AdMob banner on the archived projects list; the counter screen never shows ads. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks consent status each time the app starts, so the app can use the network before any banner appears. Google documents that its Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate general location;
  • app interactions such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostics such as launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • device identifiers such as the app set ID and, where available, identifiers of accounts signed in on the device.

Tinkrow does not request the Google Play services advertising ID permission, but the Google Mobile Ads SDK adds Android Privacy Sandbox permissions (ad ID, attribution and topics), so Google may still receive an advertising identifier on some devices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit with TLS. Project names, notes and row instructions are not added to ad requests by the app. On the phone, the app also keeps simple counts of ad events (for example requests, loads and impressions) with no record content.



When you email support

An email to support@nuvopilot.com gives us your address, your message and anything you attach. We use it only to answer you and follow up on the same issue. Mail is received and answered through Zoho Mail.

Support is AI-assisted. We may submit the text of your inquiry and information you choose to include to Anthropic's Claude, an AI service we use to understand the issue and draft a reply. This processing happens on Anthropic's systems, not on your phone. App records on your phone are never sent automatically, and mailbox messages and attachments are not forwarded automatically. Anthropic handles submitted text under its own terms and privacy policy; we do not promise a particular retention period or training setting.


Please leave out passwords, verification codes, payment details and full backup files. We keep support mail only as long as needed to resolve your question or meet a legal duty, and you can ask us to delete it by writing to the same address.

Your choices and deleting data

Archive hides a project without deleting it, and row instructions can be removed one by one. To remove everything, including the before-replace copy, clear Tinkrow's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Backup files you saved elsewhere must be deleted there.

If ad privacy options apply to you, open Settings (the ⋯ button) and choose Ad privacy options. Android also offers ad privacy settings. Deleting app data does not delete data Google already received.

Children

Tinkrow is made for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, write to support@nuvopilot.com and we will delete that correspondence.

Security

Records stay in storage that other apps cannot read. Keep exported files private, because they hold your records in plain JSON. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure.

Changes and contact

When this app's data handling changes we will update this page and its effective date before or with the app update. Contact support@nuvopilot.com with the app name and your question; you never need to send your records.
